# 0.48.0-dev.32

- **Reasoning-only stream deadline guard in bare and full mode.** Under `--time-budget`, the kernel now arms a per-round checkpoint guard on the first non-empty `ReasoningDelta` (never cutting request prefill/TTFT or streams that never reason), waiting `delay.max(RunDeadline::reasoning_grace())` until the session's next untold deadline stage (`WrapUp` or `Final`, with 3% budget grace clamped 15–30 s and capped to half the headroom above `Over`). Any visible text (`TextStart`/`TextDelta`), tool call (`ToolInputStart`/`ToolCallRequested`), or `MessageFinished` disarms the guard, and rounds started after `Final` has been told are never cut.
- **Compaction-safe cut notice and unpaired reasoning replay hygiene.** When a reasoning-only stream is stopped at a deadline stage, the step finishes as `stop`, marks the stage notified (`RunDeadline::mark_notified`), and emits a transient user-role notice (`[hya] Reasoning interrupted at budget checkpoint:`) carrying the stage notice (naming the offered delivery tool for the `report` wording) plus up to 1,200 trailing characters of reasoning after the next round's `Harness::before_round` so both step-cut and full-mode native compaction preserve it until the model's next part. Cut steps strip opaque `provider_data` from closed reasoning blocks while keeping transcript text intact, and the OpenAI Responses encoder omits unpaired reasoning items from wire replay so follow-up steps cannot inherit an unpaired reasoning ID.
