# 0.48.0-dev.13

- **One `bash` call waits at most a tenth of the time budget.** In a bare run with `--time-budget`, the wait of a call is the smaller of its `timeout` (default 300 s) and a tenth of the budget: 90 s of a 900 s budget, 180 s of 1,800 s; from 3,000 s on the default of 300 s is unchanged. When the wait ends there the command keeps running and the result is `still_running`, naming this limit; a further call can wait for it again. Before, a single call could wait as long as the model asked and hold most of a short run.
- **Bare `read` does not attach an oversized image.** An image whose attachment would exceed 2 MiB serialized (a file over about 1.5 MiB) is not attached; the text gives its type, size and the limit and says to downscale or crop it with a program and read the smaller file — the rule full mode already had. Before, a single image of several megabytes went into the next request whole and the provider sent nothing back.
- **Bare mode retries a stalled or broken stream that produced nothing.** A round whose stream stalls (no frame within the idle limit) or fails while its body is read, before the model produced anything, is retried under the same rule as other transient errors (three retries, 5/10/20 s, not into the final stage of a time budget). These failures now have their own provider error variant, `StreamInterrupted`, with the same message text; full mode treats it as before.
