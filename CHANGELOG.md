# 0.48.0-dev.30

- **OpenAI Responses routes now request encrypted reasoning content and capture terminal reasoning items.** Both `openai-response` and `openai-codex` send `"include": ["reasoning.encrypted_content"]` whenever reasoning effort is enabled, and omit it when reasoning is unset or `off` so non-reasoning models do not reject the request. The Responses stream decoder claims output order on `response.output_item.added` (`reasoning`), holds summary-only `output_item.done` items open until `response.completed` or `response.incomplete` so late `encrypted_content` in `/response/output` closes in a single `ReasoningEnd` (flushing open earlier text first to preserve replay order), skips unstarted out-of-order terminal items with a warning, and logs a warning when `/response/input_transformations` reports removed reasoning items (such as `prefix_mismatch`).
- **Both modes detect dropped replayed reasoning and warn the model once per turn.** When the serving route replays a prior step's reasoning part (`>= 1,000` known reasoning tokens) and the next non-mutating round's prompt grows by less than half of that reasoning — excluding model switches, compaction/rewrites, sent-part eviction changes, tool-result image rounds, and transient notice rounds — the kernel logs `reasoning_replay_dropped` and appends one transient user-role notice advising the model to keep key derivations, findings, and plans in visible text, workspace files, or the todo list.
